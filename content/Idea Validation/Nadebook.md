## Unique Proposition

Currently, you can practice in-game in the following ways:
- Paid Practice Servers (https://refrag.gg/)
	- Pros
		- Package of features besides just nade practice
	- Cons
		- Paid
		- Flows are not optimized for nade practice, not convinient
- Custom Workshop Maps (https://yprac.com/)
	- Pros
		- Free
	- Cons
		- Not possible to add your own lineups
- Simply rewatching video in-game and running through the map
	- Pros
		- Free
	- Cons
		- Time consuming
		- Need to reopen videos every time

Nadebook is a free tool anyone can use without the need of third party services / servers / maps. It will be fully tailored to address nade-practicing scenario.
