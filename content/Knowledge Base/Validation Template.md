## Problem Evidence
- What painful job-to-be-done am I solving, and for whom?
- What current, real evidence do I have (my experience, friends, interviews)?
## Market Shape
- Trend signals
- Similar products and their state. Do people pay for this, do companies exit?
- Products that received funding
- Current top alternatives and my differentiation
## Other
- Do I have specific skills, access, or insight that give me an edge here?
- Would I happily work on this for extended period of time?
- Why now?
- Where do these users already hang out (communities, search terms, partners, tools)?
- SAAS vs Ads, check preliminary pricing or ad revenue based on number of customers
- How fast can I make MVP
## Key Risks
- Why can it fail right away / Why it was not solved already?
- Review all assumption above and think of why it can be wrong

# Competion
- Why do users churn/complain about each? Where are they under‑serving the beachhead?
- For which tight use case can I be 10× easier/faster (not just “better”)?
- What is the switching cost and my migration plan?
# Offer
- Dream outcome I promise, and earliest proof I can show?
- How do I increase perceived likelihood (guarantee, case studies, pilot milestones)?
- How do I reduce time delay & effort (concierge start, done‑for‑you, templates)?
- Who pays, from which budget, and what pricing model fits (SaaS/usage/commission/ads)?
# Scalability & Fundability
- Do similar companies receive funding?
