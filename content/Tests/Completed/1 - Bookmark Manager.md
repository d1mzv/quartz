# Review

## What went well
- First launched product
	- Did Concept, Design, Branding, Development, UX, Subscriptions, Launch, Ads, Metrics
- Pivoted to a smaller niche/scope
	- From "Second brain in your phone" broad product to a more narrow "Bookmark Manager" app
- I made something I consider beautiful and convenient
## What needs to be better

- Much better research
	- A lot more initial validation should be done before starting development
- Niche is still wide and not very clear
	- "Bookmark manager" is quite specific term. At the same time use case itself is very broad and goes against the principle on targeting more narrow niche initially
- MVP scope should have been smaller still
	- Mobile App + Extension + Web App is too many things before any validation
- Interviews should be more structured and not just "would you use this tool"
	- Excitement from friends and family doesn't mean they will use the tool in the end. No one knows if they will use it or they just like idea of using it
